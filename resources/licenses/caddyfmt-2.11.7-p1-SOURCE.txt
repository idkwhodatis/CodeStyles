CodeStyles Caddy formatter-only WASM adapter, candidate 2.11.7-p1.
This is not an official Caddy distribution. It is unpublished and disabled.

Caddy upstream version: 2.11.7
Source commit: 72dd0fb067f6d7826c7f79907670ba4a713bfe37
https://github.com/caddyserver/caddy/tree/72dd0fb067f6d7826c7f79907670ba4a713bfe37

The exact formatter.go and formatter_test.go are retained. heredoc.go extracts
only the unchanged heredocMarkerRegexp declaration from lexer.go and records
that extraction. No Caddy server or external Go modules are compiled.
Complete adapter source, source pins and offline build scripts accompany the
candidate under sources/. The CodeStyles adapter uses the MIT License.
